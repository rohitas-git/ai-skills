# Ethical review structure

Optional structure for `/1-thinking-ethical`; it does not add evidence, law, consent, risk-acceptance
authority, research permission, or an implementation workflow.

```text
Action/policy and version:
Decision owner and affected scope:
Evidence boundary and unknowns:
Values, rights, and duties:
Option:
  observed/supplied effects:
  predicted harms and benefits:
  who benefits / who bears the burden:
  power, consent, refusal, appeal, and redress:
  safeguards and residual harm:
  confidence / disconfirming evidence:
Strongest grounded dissent:
Conditional recommendation or fair decision process:
Monitoring and stop/review conditions:
```

Use the same considerations for each feasible option. Include non-action only when it is a meaningful
choice. If values cannot honestly be reconciled, expose the conflict and propose a fair process
instead of inventing consensus. Do not infer sensitive traits or describe a possible benefit, harm,
group preference, legality, or consent as established evidence.
