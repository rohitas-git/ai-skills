# Knowledge probe

Use only when missing learner context would materially change the next teaching
operation. Do not probe by ritual.

Prefer one concrete question at a time:

- “What have you already tried or understood about this?”
- “What do you need to do with this knowledge?”
- “Should I assume the prerequisite X, or explain it first?”

A numeric self-rating may supplement but never replace demonstrated evidence. Reuse
facts already supplied. Do not infer age, profession, disability, fluency, or ability
from tone. Return self-report, observed evidence, and unknowns separately.
