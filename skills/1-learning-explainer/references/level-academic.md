# Academic audience guide

Use formal terminology and prerequisite concepts appropriate to the named field and
level; do not treat “academic” as permission to fabricate citations.

- Define the object and framework precisely.
- Explain relevant mechanism/method and evidentiary basis.
- Distinguish consensus, competing interpretations, methodological limits, and unknowns.
- Cite only sources actually consulted or supplied; current/disputed claims require
  the normal research workflow.
- Include equations, history, or open questions only when they serve the request.

Rigor means traceable claims, not maximal length.
