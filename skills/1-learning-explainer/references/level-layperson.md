# General-audience guide

Assume general knowledge but no specialist vocabulary.

- Lead with what the concept is and why it matters to the stated goal.
- Introduce only necessary domain terms and explain them in place.
- Connect mechanism to one real consequence or example when helpful.
- Separate established fact, simplification, inference, and open question.
- Avoid both jargon dumping and confident oversimplification.

Do not add a “bigger picture,” analogy, or misconception section by default.
