# Frontier audience guide

Frontier explanations require expert prerequisites and current, primary evidence.

- Pin the field, time boundary, and exact disputed/open question.
- Separate established baseline, recent evidence, active disagreement, and inference.
- Prefer primary papers, standards, or authoritative first-party sources; state search
  and coverage limits.
- Avoid “breakthrough,” “state of the art,” priority, or recency claims without dated
  support.
- Surface negative results, replication limits, and unresolved alternatives when
  material.

If current research was not supplied or authorized, return the stable conceptual
baseline and route the exact current-evidence question to `1-research`.
