# One-layer deep dive

Select one layer that answers the user's exact follow-up:

- **mechanism:** how the parts produce the result;
- **formalism:** notation, equation, or precise model;
- **edge case:** where ordinary behavior changes;
- **limitation:** where a claim/model stops holding;
- **evidence:** what supports or challenges it;
- **connection:** how it relates to one named adjacent concept.

Preserve the prior audience and definitions unless explicitly changing them. Add only
one layer per invocation, avoid repeating the whole explanation, and stop after
returning it. Current/disputed evidence routes to `1-research`; “deep” alone does not
authorize tools.
