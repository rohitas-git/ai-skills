# Six Thinking Hats lens guide

Optional method for `/1-perspective-analysis` when the user wants complementary thinking modes
rather than claims about stakeholder views. These lenses add structure, not evidence, stakeholder
representation, research authority, or permission to expand the fixed decision.

## Passes

1. **Opening Blue:** restate the fixed decision, evidence boundary, purpose, relevant modes, and order.
2. **White:** separate supplied observations, reports, assumptions, conflicts, and material unknowns.
3. **Red:** include only emotions that were reported; generated emotional reactions must be labeled
   hypothetical and cannot stand in for user or stakeholder research.
4. **Black:** identify plausible downside mechanisms, affected parties, confidence, and safeguards.
5. **Yellow:** identify plausible benefit mechanisms, beneficiaries, required conditions, and confidence.
6. **Green:** generate decision-relevant alternatives; mark them as proposals rather than evidence.
7. **Closing Blue:** synthesize only prior passes, conflicts, decision options, owners, and next evidence.

Use all passes when a full Six Thinking Hats analysis was explicitly selected. Otherwise omit modes
that do not help the decision; do not pad the response to complete a ritual. Risk-focused or
decision-focused subsets are valid when named honestly.

## Evidence rules

- No mode may manufacture facts, stakeholder sentiment, consensus, consent, or authority.
- Keep observations, reported views, assumptions, hypotheses, and generated alternatives distinct.
- The closing Blue pass introduces no new evidence and does not turn the lenses into a vote.
- A negative and positive pass are not proof of balance; weight claims by evidence and consequence.
