# Specialist handoff schema

Adapt this structure; do not treat it as authority to start the specialist.

```text
Caller and decision:
Candidate: repo/worktree/branch/HEAD; working-tree inclusion; baseline
Scope: included paths and explicit exclusions
Axis: one review concern and owner skill
Evidence: required sources/checks and cutoff
Sampling: deterministic inclusions, risk-based sample, blind spots
Authority: read-only; allowed tools/network; no publication or fixes
Return: findings format, coverage limits, candidate identity
```
