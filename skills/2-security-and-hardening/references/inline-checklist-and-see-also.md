# Compact checkpoint

Use for a concise summary after scoped hardening, not as another full audit.

- What authorized change, threat and candidate were addressed?
- What denied and allowed behavior was actually verified?
- Which controls/checks are not applicable, blocked or still uncertain?
- Were unrelated files, local services and production state preserved?
- What evidence and remaining decision return to the caller?

The detailed [security checklist](./security-checklist.md) owns verification depth.
Do not duplicate its requirements, impose numeric password/rate defaults, or treat
an unchecked box as permission to expand the PR.
