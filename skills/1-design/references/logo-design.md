# One-logo guidance

Start from the pinned brand brief and intended uses. Choose one fitting mark structure (wordmark, lettermark, pictorial,
abstract, emblem, or combination) rather than producing a brand system. Favor distinctive, reproducible geometry;
verify small-size silhouette, monochrome behavior, contrast, spacing, spelling, and the exact vector/raster format.
Color associations are contextual hypotheses, not universal facts. Do not claim trademark clearance; report that as a
separate legal check. One logo request does not authorize variants, stationery, a style guide, or publication.
