Pin the exact placement, dimensions, audience, copy, supplied assets, rights, and brand contract. Preserve faces,
logos, products, and factual claims when editing; do not infer consent or representation rights. Keep essential content
inside crop-safe areas and verify legibility at displayed size, contrast, alt-text recommendation, output format, and
compression. Platform conventions change; verify material requirements from current first-party documentation.
