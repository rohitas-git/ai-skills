# Banner constraint guidance

- Treat the user's exact placement and dimensions as authoritative.
- If dimensions are absent and materially affect the result, obtain them or verify current requirements from the
  platform's first-party documentation; do not rely on remembered platform tables.
- Reserve crop/safe areas for responsive or placement-dependent use and keep essential copy/logo content inside them.
- Check legibility at actual display size, contrast, file size/format, and print bleed/color mode when applicable.
- Style labels are descriptive prompts, not evidence that a style performs better. Never claim CTR, reach, or policy
  compliance without current measured or authoritative evidence.
