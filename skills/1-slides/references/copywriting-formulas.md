# Evidence-safe slide copy

- One clear point per slide; headlines state the takeaway when evidence supports it.
- Prefer concrete nouns, active verbs, and traceable numbers over hype.
- Keep source citations next to material claims and distinguish estimate, inference, and measured fact.
- AIDA, PAS, FAB, or cost-of-inaction structures are optional rhetorical tools, never permission to exaggerate pain,
  urgency, scarcity, social proof, or outcomes.
- Preserve the user's terminology, legal qualifiers, and unknowns. Do not manufacture testimonials, customer counts,
  benchmarks, savings, or calls to action.
