# Technical blog review guide

Review in order of likely reader impact. A clean dimension needs one short sentence, not manufactured findings.

1. **Reader promise and structure:** Does the opening identify the problem and what the reader will learn? Does each section build on what came before? Are transitions, length, asides, and the conclusion useful?
2. **Technical trust:** Check code syntax and context when a safe, authorized check is available. Check terms, API versions, factual claims, and citations against primary sources. Distinguish a demonstrated error from a question needing verification.
3. **Writing:** Find confusing passages, repetition, vague claims, unexplained jargon, inconsistent voice, and needless padding. Preserve intentional conversational language, fragments, and passive voice when they work.
4. **Publication details:** Inspect available frontmatter, title and description, dates, related links, link text, and tutorial steps. Do not require fields a publisher does not use.

For each important issue, give a location or brief excerpt, why it affects the reader, and a concrete edit or check. Prioritize misunderstanding, broken examples, unsupported claims, and lost reader momentum above taste. Keep the high-priority list short when the post has no major problems. A readiness verdict should state what was verified and what remains uncertain.
