# Steel-manning technique guide

Optional techniques for `/1-thinking-steel-manning`. They do not expand the fixed position,
authorize research or tools, represent a real person/group, or require rebuttal/synthesis.

## Construction checks

- State the target and actual alternative without loaded wording.
- Separate explicit reasons from charitable inferred rationale.
- Use the strongest available evidence and constraints, not invented facts.
- Name the legitimate concern or objective that makes the opposition worth considering.
- Include a concrete alternative when one exists; do not manufacture a false binary.
- State assumptions, applicability, evidence limits, confidence, and overturn conditions.

## Failure patterns

- **Straw man:** weaker than the supplied opposing case.
- **Mind-reading:** attributes motives or beliefs without evidence.
- **False balance:** defends factual error, harm, or a hard violation for symmetry.
- **Proxy representation:** generated text is presented as a group's real view.
- **Automatic debate:** continues into rebuttal or synthesis beyond the requested output.
- **Same-source corroboration:** treats restated shared evidence as independent support.

## Optional output

```text
Target position and strongest faithful restatement:
Opposing position:
Core legitimate concern:
Strongest opposing case:
Evidence / assumptions / limits:
What weakens or overturns it:
Valid points preserved from each side:
Engagement or synthesis: only if requested
```

Rigor comes from fidelity and evidence, not harshness, verbosity, disagreement, or forced balance.
