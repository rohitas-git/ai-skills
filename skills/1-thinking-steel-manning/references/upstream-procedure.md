# Upstream steel-manning provenance

Adapted from `tjboudreaux/cc-thinking-skills` `thinking-steel-manning` at
`7b8fece345dfaa11773be7152ccd194589cb5437` (repository marked MIT at ingestion).

Retained ideas:

- engage the strongest faithful opposing case, not a caricature;
- surface the legitimate concern;
- state evidence and overturn conditions;
- update when the countercase succeeds.

Changed locally:

- steel-manning no longer automatically triggers because agreement seems likely;
- constructing the case is separated from rebuttal, synthesis, critique, and implementation;
- generated opposition is not presented as a real person's or group's view;
- factual errors, unsafe acts, and hard violations do not receive fabricated defenses.

This file records provenance and adaptation decisions. It is not an independent workflow or authority.
