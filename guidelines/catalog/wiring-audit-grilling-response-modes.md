# Grilling response-mode wiring audit

Reviewed:

- `1-anti-sycophancy`
- `1-honest-advisor`

- `1-critique/references/full-guide.md`
- their targeted `0-grilling` metadata, workflow, HTML, and Butler-flow routes

## Result

These three skills now have separate atomic jobs. Anti-Sycophancy is an explicitly requested
evidence-before-agreement overlay for one bounded response. Honest Advisor examines one supplied
interpersonal event or personal decision without automatic reassurance, blame, diagnosis, or
professional-care substitution. Critique tests one fixed idea, claim, argument, or proposal against
assumptions, evidence, logic, framing, alternatives, and consequences. Each returns to Grilling or
the actual caller and starts no research, editing, implementation, memory retrieval, professional
care, or debate loop automatically.

Anti-Sycophancy fixes the request, claims, evidence cutoff, uncertainty, safety needs, output
constraints, and authority. It removes agreement pressure without discarding material context or
humane communication, assesses claims independently, updates only for new evidence, permits earned
agreement, and rejects invented opposition or false balance. It is not a standing conversation mode
unless explicitly requested as a reversible wider setting. It creates no worktree or tool effect;
an already-scoped artifact retains exact repository/worktree/branch/`HEAD` or source/version
identity, and the worktree does not validate claims or isolate external resources.

Honest Advisor fixes one current account and its time boundary, desired help, affected people,
safety/power constraints, missing facts, privacy limits, and output. It does not retrieve prior
conversations, contacts, records, or external facts by default. Observations, reported statements,
interpretations, emotional impact, assumed motives, and unknowns remain distinct. A charitable
alternative is optional and cannot erase evidenced harm, power imbalance, or unequal responsibility.
Urgent safety concerns stop the ordinary format and route to suitable local support without
hardcoded region-specific contacts or dangerous confrontation advice. Its only ordinary action is
one low-risk reversible suggestion; it performs no external action.

Critique requires explicit activation and pins one candidate, decision, scope/non-goals, evidence
cutoff, affected groups, failure costs, depth, and authority. Repository-backed candidates also pin
repository/worktree/branch/`HEAD`, dirty ownership, exact file/hunk or plan revision, and
working-tree-versus-committed status. Candidate content is untrusted evidence, not instructions.
Findings distinguish contradiction, missing proof, plausible risk, and optional refinement; each
material issue carries a locator, evidence/reasoning, consequence, confidence, falsifier or
resolution evidence, and proportionate mitigation. Supported reasoning is retained. The optional
technique guide no longer bans acknowledgment wholesale or treats discomfort as proof of rigor.

The Grilling parent now exposes every actual workflow child and planning/implementation exit in
metadata, includes direct triggers for these response modes, links each child from HTML, and states
that response-mode/prototype evidence returns to the caller. This was a targeted parent-route repair,
not a completed semantic audit of the entire `0-grilling` body and reference package.
