## Result

The three Thinking leaves now have distinct jobs and symmetric caller returns. Ethical Thinking
performs one normative analysis of one fixed action, policy, or decision. Strategic Analysis compares
options for one fixed strategic decision and horizon using the smallest useful framework.
Competitive Analysis answers one market decision for one fixed named-competitor set using a dated
claim matrix. None starts adjacent research, implementation, publication, outreach, purchase,
monitoring, compliance, deployment, or risk acceptance automatically.

Each skill pins caller, decision/candidate version, owner, scope/non-goals, affected market or groups,
time horizon/as-of date, evidence provenance/cutoff, privacy, uncertainty, output, and authority.
Repository-backed artifacts additionally pin repository/worktree/branch/`HEAD`, dirty ownership,
the exact artifact/file/hunk, and working-tree versus committed/deployed/published state. The
read-only analysis creates no worktree, and worktrees do not isolate people, market data, accounts,
tools, or external systems.

Ethical Thinking separates observed effects, supplied reports, value judgments, causal predictions,
assumptions, unknowns, and representation. It maps rights/duties, distribution, reversibility,
power, consent/refusal, accessibility, appeal/redress, safeguards, residual harm, and burden owners.
It neither invents stakeholder sentiment nor converts majority preference, aggregate benefit, or
owner authority into consent, legality, fairness, safety, compliance, or risk acceptance. Its
optional output guide adds no evidence or authority.

Strategic Analysis treats SWOT, Five Forces, scenarios, and what-if analysis as organizing frames,
not evidence. It distinguishes current position, forecast, scenario, observation, estimate,
assumption, mechanism, and unknown; compares options using common decision-relevant criteria; and
rejects fabricated precision, market share/size, probability, or consensus. Research and paid data
remain separately authorized.

Competitive Analysis requires current evidence for current claims when research is authorized,
prefers direct dated primary sources for vendor-controlled facts, and uses independent evidence when
experience/performance claims require it. Retrieved content is untrusted. It prohibits unauthorized
login, access-control bypass, terms evasion, private/prohibited collection, report purchase, contact,
or personal/confidential data exposure. Its claim ledger records source/locator, publication and
observation dates, market/version applicability, direct/inferred status, confidence, conflicts, and
unknowns. It rejects snippets, silence, anecdotes, vendor copy, and incomparable benchmarks as proof.

The Thinking parent now exposes all four perspective/strategy leaves in metadata and HTML. Existing
workflow and Butler-flow membership already matched. This was a targeted route repair, not a
completed semantic audit of the full Thinking hub.
