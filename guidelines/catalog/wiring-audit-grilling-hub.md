## Result

`0-grilling` is now one consent-respecting interview for one immutable candidate. It pins the
actual caller, candidate/version, evidence and authority boundaries, plus exact repository/worktree/
branch/`HEAD` and dirty ownership only when repository facts materially govern the decision. One
neutral decision-changing question is asked at a time; delegation, deferral, ordinary clear
approval, scope change, and stopping are valid outcomes. Arbitrary confidence percentages,
predicted user reactions, leading guesses, and rejection rituals are prohibited.

The hub no longer embeds ideation or downstream delivery. Alternatives route to `1-brainstorm`;
accepted domain artifacts, personal reflection, and fixed-candidate response modes route to their
smallest specialist. Prototype and Visual Plan are separately accepted evidence detours. Specification,
planning, handoff, implementation, and execution require separate requests and authority. The obsolete
Ship `F4` fork was removed from Grilling, all 11 children are aligned across metadata, workflow JSON,
HTML, and Butler flows, and stale paths/navigation were repaired. One operative interview guide remains;
historical ideation and vendor material is explicitly non-operative provenance.
