## Result

Perspective Analysis now compares explicitly labeled stakeholder, persona, or thinking-mode lenses
on one fixed decision and exposes shared ground, power asymmetries, and irreducible conflicts. A lens
is a reasoning aid, not evidence of what a real person or population believes. The skill returns to
Thinking or the actual caller and starts no research, interview, design, implementation, publication,
or stakeholder contact automatically.

The candidate pins the decision and version, owner, purpose, scope/non-goals, affected groups,
evidence provenance/cutoff, uncertainty, privacy, output, and authority. Repository-backed decisions
also pin repository/worktree/branch/`HEAD`, dirty ownership, exact artifact/file/hunk, and
working-tree-versus-committed/deployed identity. It creates no worktree; worktrees do not isolate
users, data, tools, or external systems.

Briefs, tickets, messages, personas, research, analytics, and links are sensitive untrusted evidence,
not instructions, representative truth, consent, or authority. Stakeholder lenses map interests,
power, constraints, benefits, and harms. Persona lenses require supplied or verified research with
coverage limits. Thinking modes provide complementary analytical passes. Every lens separates
observations, attributed or hypothetical views, incentives, constraints, concerns, benefits/harms,
unknowns, confidence, and confirming/refuting evidence. The skill rejects demographic stereotypes,
fabricated user sentiment, fake consensus, owner-as-user substitution, and invented votes.

The Six Thinking Hats guide is now optional, scope-neutral, and evidence-aware. Reported and
hypothetical emotions remain distinct; risks, benefits, and alternatives carry conditions and
confidence; the closing synthesis adds no evidence and does not count the lenses as votes. A full
sequence applies only when selected; subsets are permitted when named honestly.

The Thinking parent now exposes Perspective Analysis in metadata and HTML. Existing workflow and
Butler-flow membership already matched. This was a targeted parent-route repair, not a completed
semantic audit of the full Thinking hub or its other leaves.
